## Lamplit

[`lamplit/`](lamplit/) is a silent online study game that runs in the browser. Study in a sunlit library,
a cozy coffee shop or a neon net café, and walk out to the town square between them for a break. Pick a
desk, turn on your lamp and focus alongside everyone else. Or open the world map and study inside the
Great Pyramid, the Colosseum, a temple in Japan or the Sagrada Família, then step outside to look
around. Earn lumens while you focus and spend them on clothes, pets and more. No chat; play as a guest
or sign up. See [lamplit/README.md](lamplit/README.md).
